
# Oxford–Japan Plant Conservation Partnership (OJPCP)

**Advancing botanical knowledge, biodiversity research and plant conservation in Japan through international scientific collaboration.**

## About the Partnership

The Oxford–Japan Plant Conservation Partnership (OJPCP) is a collaborative initiative coordinated through the University of Oxford Botanic Garden and Arboretum. It brings together botanical research, biodiversity informatics, fieldwork and conservation science to improve knowledge and understanding of Japan's vascular flora.

The partnership supports the documentation, study and conservation of plant diversity through international scientific cooperation, open research and the development of accessible biodiversity resources.

## Vascular Plants of Japan Database (VPJD)

The **Vascular Plants of Japan Database (VPJD)** is a flagship initiative of OJPCP, developing an integrated, reproducible resource for Japan's vascular plant diversity.

The database brings together taxonomic, geographic and occurrence information to support botanical research, conservation assessments, spatial biodiversity analysis and the management of living collections.

## Research and Conservation Priorities

OJPCP supports work across five interconnected areas:

1. **Taxonomy and biodiversity informatics** — developing authoritative, reproducible botanical datasets.
2. **Plant distributions and biogeography** — improving geographic knowledge of Japan's vascular flora.
3. **Species and habitat conservation** — supporting threatened plant assessments, Key Biodiversity Areas and conservation planning.
4. **Botanical fieldwork and data mobilisation** — improving the capture, standardisation and sharing of botanical observations.
5. **Living collections and ex situ conservation** — linking field research, plant provenance and conservation collections.

## Open Science and Data Stewardship

OJPCP promotes transparent methods, reproducible workflows, responsible biodiversity data management and appropriate attribution.

This GitHub organisation provides a collaborative home for research code, data-processing pipelines, documentation and selected scientific outputs.

Sensitive species-locality information, unpublished research data and restricted collaborative materials will be managed in accordance with applicable data-sharing and conservation requirements.

## Collaboration

OJPCP welcomes scientific exchange with botanical institutions, researchers, conservation practitioners and biodiversity data initiatives in Japan and internationally.
